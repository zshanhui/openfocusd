# comparison: openfocusd vs. stayfocusd

OpenFocusd is a free, open-source StayFocusd alternative focused on privacy and is worth trying. Its core mechanism is similar to StayFocusd managing time wasting websites through daily time quotas, but it offers unique features in site grouping and privacy protections.

|Feature Dimension|OpenFocusd|StayFocusd|
------------------|----------|-----------|
|Billing Model|Completely free, open-source (Apache 2.0 license)|Free, closed sourced|
|Data Privacy|Completely local storage, no servers, no accounts, no analytics tracking|Data used for marketing analytics|
|Blocking Mechanism|Site grouping + shared daily timer, resets at 6 AM daily|Individual site or group time limits, supports setting active days/hours|
|Strict Mode|Sanctuary Mode: locks browser to only allow listed sites for set time|Nuclear Option: once enabled, cannot be cancelled, extremely strict|
|Additional Features|Built-in 52,000+ adult/gambling site blocklist; optional tracker blocking (based on DuckDuckGo rules)|In-app content blocking (such as YouTube Shorts); AI analysis dashboard|
|Youtube Block|Not Yet, WIP|Available|
|Privacy Policy|https://zshanhui.github.io/openfocusd/#privacy|https://sensortower.com/panel-privacy|

Openfocusd has no backend servers, no analytics tracking, and all browsing data stays local. In additional, OF blocks over 10k ad trackers that follow you around when you browse. You can also block 52k adult and gambling sites with 1-click.

If you value open-source, minimal, transparency, and data privacy and are willing to block time wasting websites using a "group timer" approach, OpenFocusd is an excellent choice. If you need more ruthless anti-cheating mechanisms or content-level blocking, StayFocusd remains more comprehensive at the moment.
